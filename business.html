Lagos Businesses Adjust to New Digital Payment Rules

By Daily Brief Staff · October 7, 2026

Businesses in Lagos are reviewing their digital payment processes as financial institutions and technology providers continue to expand electronic payment services.

Small and medium-sized businesses have increasingly adopted mobile payment platforms, online banking and other digital services as customers seek faster and more convenient ways to make transactions.

Industry observers say businesses should continue to pay attention to announcements from regulators and financial institutions as requirements for electronic transactions evolve.

Companies are also reviewing their internal procedures, particularly around transaction records, customer information and access to financial systems.
